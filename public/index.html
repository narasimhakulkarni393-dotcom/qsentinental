Quantum Variational Classifier (VQC) Lab

Exploring Hilbert space feature encoding for non-linear cyber decision boundaries

Q-Sentinel implements a Variational Quantum Classifier mapped through Pauli-rotation angle encodings (R_y(θ), R_z(θ)). Lexical features (such as is_ip_address, num_dots, num_subdomains) are transformed into rotational parameters in a simulated quantum circuit with parameterized entangling CNOT layers.
